# Privacy Policy (Datenschutz)

**App:** Notires  
**Effective date:** 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Notires (“the App”) handles information when you use it on your device.

## 1. Introduction

Notires helps you track food expiry dates by scanning package labels and storing items on your device. This notice describes what data is processed, how it is used, and what choices you have.

## 2. Who we are

The App is provided by **sa25** (© 2026 sa25).

For privacy questions, contact us through the App Store or Google Play listing for Notires.

## 3. Data we process

The App is designed to work **on your device**. In the current version, your food data is **not uploaded** to our servers (we do not operate a cloud backend for Notires inventory data).

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be stored **locally** on your device:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Food inventory | Product name, expiration date, reminder date, creation date |
| Photos | Images of food package labels you capture or select |
| Settings | Language, reminder time, days-before-expiry, calendar preferences |
| System identifiers (local) | Local notification IDs; optional calendar event IDs created by the App |

The App does **not** require an account and does **not** collect a name, email address, or payment details inside the App.

## 4. How we use it

We process the data above solely to provide App features:

- Display and manage your food inventory
- Read text from labels using **on-device OCR** (for example Apple Vision on iOS / ML Kit on Android)
- Schedule **local** expiry reminders
- Optionally create or remove **calendar** events or alarms you choose to add
- Remember your preferences (language, reminder defaults)

OCR runs on your device. In the current version, label images and extracted text are **not sent** to sa25 for cloud processing.

## 5. Permissions

The App may ask for the following device permissions. You can deny or revoke them in system settings; some features will then be unavailable.

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Camera | Scan food package labels |
| Photo library | Choose an existing photo to scan |
| Notifications | Local reminders before items expire |
| Calendar | Optional expiry-related calendar events or alarms |

## 6. Sharing and third parties

**We do not sell your personal data.**

In the current version, inventory data and photos stay on your device. Processing may involve:

- **Apple or Google** — operating-system features used for OCR, notifications, calendar, and device security
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — distribution and store-related services under their own terms and privacy policies

A future optional cloud AI label-reading feature (for example via Firebase) is **not enabled** in the current version. If that changes, this policy will be updated before it is offered.

## 7. Storage and security

Food items, settings, and photos are stored in the App’s local storage on your device. Security also depends on your device lock, OS updates, and how you protect your phone or tablet.

## 8. Your choices

You can:

- Edit or delete individual food items (and related photos, reminders, and calendar events the App created)
- Change or clear settings inside the App
- Revoke camera, photos, notifications, or calendar access in system settings
- Uninstall the App, which typically removes locally stored App data according to your operating system

Because inventory data is not held by us on a server, we cannot remotely retrieve or erase a copy of your on-device inventory.

## 9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
